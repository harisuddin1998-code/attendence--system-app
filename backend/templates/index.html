How it works

From classroom to confirmed, in four steps

No live camera feeds, no server-side video infrastructure to install or maintain — just two phone photos and an AI model that does the rest.

1

Teacher opens the app

Logs into the Monitor teacher app and picks the class or course about to be marked present.

2

Capture the room

Two quick photos — left half, right half — cover every student in the class, however the room is arranged.

3

AI recognizes every face

Each face is matched in real time against the student's one-time face registration — no manual roll call, no scanning ID cards.

4

Students are notified instantly

The moment a face is confirmed, that student gets a push notification with the time, class, and their own matched photo.
