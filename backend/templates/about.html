About Us

The developer behind Monitor

Monitor is built and maintained by a single computer science student who wanted attendance software that actually respects a teacher's time — not another system that adds more work than it saves.

Who built this

Shaikh Haris Uddin

Haris is a Software Developer and technology enthusiast with a genuine passion for building practical software that solves real problems — not just technically impressive demos. He currently works as a Senior Software Developer at Origin Solutions, where he applies professional software engineering practices to real production systems, experience that shows in how carefully Monitor is put together end to end — from the face-recognition pipeline down to how a teacher taps through a class in seconds.

His academic journey began at Hamdard Public School, where he completed his Matriculation, and continues today as a BSCS student at Iqra University, North Campus — where Monitor itself was designed and built. Along the way he's built a genuinely broad skill set: Python, Flask, OpenCV, DeepFace, PHP, JavaScript, MySQL, Machine Learning, and Computer Vision all show up across his project history, and Monitor draws on nearly all of them at once.

What stands out most is the range: mobile apps, AI-powered vision systems, and full production web platforms, all shipped by one person who clearly enjoys turning a hard problem into something people can actually use.

Our vision

Technology should not just digitize a process — it should make it better

That's the standard Monitor is held to. Every attendance session it processes is meant to give a teacher back minutes they'd otherwise lose to a roll call, and give students instant, reliable confirmation instead of uncertainty. It's a small problem in the grand scheme of software — but it's one Haris chose to solve properly, end to end, rather than leave half-built. That attention to detail — from a working AI pipeline down to a red-and-white icon on your home screen — is exactly why Monitor is worth trusting with something as routine, and as important, as attendance.
